Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

HitClipBox ("we") runs an internal tool that publishes short videos to HitClipBox's own social media channels. This policy explains what data the tool uses.

Who uses the app

The HitClipBox app is used only by the HitClipBox team to manage HitClipBox's own channels. It is not offered to the public and does not sign in other users.

Google / YouTube data

When we connect our own YouTube channel, the app requests these Google permissions:

  • youtube.upload – to upload videos to the HitClipBox channel.
  • youtube.readonly – to confirm which channel is connected and read basic channel information.

The app stores only the OAuth access/refresh token on our own computer, protected by file permissions. It does not store passwords, does not access any other account's data, and does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone. Data is used only to provide the upload feature described above.

HitClipBox's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

TikTok and Instagram

If we connect our own TikTok or Instagram accounts, the app uses the official APIs only to publish videos to those HitClipBox accounts and to read their basic profile and post status. Tokens are stored locally in the same protected way.

Public stream data

To find highlights, the tool reads public live-stream chat message rates anonymously. We do not store chat users' identities or build profiles of viewers; message counts are kept only in memory.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until we disconnect an account. You can revoke the app's access to a Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To request deletion of any data or removal of a clip, email hitclipbox@gmail.com.

Children

Our services are not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change.
